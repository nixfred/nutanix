The 3-week war plan (deadline July 1)

WK1 · Jun 9-15Foundations. Legend + K8s crash course + kind lab reps (days 1-3). Guides 01-02 + NKPA course modules 1-4 (days 4-7). Flashcards daily from day one.
WK2 · Jun 16-22The platform. Guides 03-04 + NKPA modules 5-8. Nutanix Test Drive hands-on: Kommander, workspaces, projects, platform apps. Section quiz after each guide; misses feed the drill bucket.
WK3 · Jun 23-30Exam mode. Mock #1 cold by Jun 24 (preserves a retake window). Rebuild weak sections. Official Nutanix practice exam as the calibration gate. Mock #2 by Jun 28; at 70%+ book and sit it Jun 29-30.
Free official prep: the Nutanix Kubernetes Platform Administration (NKPA) course is free, 8 modules, and tracks the blueprint nearly 1:1. It is the spine; this package is the muscle. Register for the exam at Nutanix University.
