Retakes (know before you book)

  • 3 paid attempts total, 7-day wait between each.
  • After attempt 3: 60-day lockout (or email university@nutanix.com for a reset).
  • With a July 1 deadline: first attempt by June 23-24 preserves one retake window. Do not book the first attempt on June 30.

Delivery

  • Remote proctored: locked-down browser session, webcam, clean desk, no second monitor, government ID. Test your machine + network the day before.
  • Test center: arrive 15 minutes early with government ID.
Integrity: Nutanix revokes certifications for exam security violations (copying questions, study material during the exam, false identity). Braindump familiarity is also how Customers lose trust in a badge. We built this package clean for a reason.

Final 48 hours

  • Mock exam score 70%+ consistently → book it. Below that, drill the weakest section, not your strongest.
  • Rebuild your cheat sheet (one page of personal weak facts) and review it the morning of. The vmik.net candidates swear by this.
  • Re-run the flashcards in drill-only mode until the drill bucket is empty.
  • Skim the glossary and the four "in one breath" boxes at the end of each study guide.

In the room

  • 96 sec/question average; bank time on the gimmes. Flag and move on anything over 2 minutes.
  • Multi-response: the count is stated. Pick exactly that many; partial logic = eliminate the absurd two first.
  • Translate VMware-brain to Nutanix vocabulary BEFORE answering (the legend).
  • Scenario verbs are the blueprint's verbs: "determine," "given a use case," "troubleshoot." The answer is usually the OPERATIONAL one, not the cleverest one.
  • License tier tells: attach external / Insights / multi-tenant ⇒ Ultimate. Air gap tells: registry first, trust the CA, seed before upgrade.
